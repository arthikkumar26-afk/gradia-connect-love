# Gradia Connect Love

Design and build a professional, warm, and lovable website for Gradia — a consultancy that connects candidates with software companies and educational colleges.

Requirements:

Pages: Create 50+ fully designed pages (see sitemap below).

Interactivity: Every option, button, menu, and link must be clickable and must display relevant content — no placeholders or “coming soon.”

Navigation: Sticky header with logo, navigation (Home, For Candidates, For Employers, Resources, About, Contact), CTAs (Apply Now / Post Job), search, dark/light toggle, and language selector. Global footer with quick links, newsletter signup, contact info, and social icons.

Candidates section: Job listings with filters, job detail pages, apply flows, dashboards, resume builder, mock interview scheduler, salary insights, career coaching pages. After clicking any job, show full details and application steps. After clicking “Apply,” show multi-step form with upload, skills, preferences, and review.

Employers section: Job posting wizard, employer dashboard, shortlist management, candidate messaging, packages & pricing, case studies, institutional partnerships. After clicking “Post Job,” show full multi-step form with preview & publish.

Resources: Blog, events, webinars, resource library, downloads, video testimonials. Each item opens to a full detail page with share, save, and related content.

Admin section: Role-based dashboards, user/job management, analytics reports, CRM integrations, audit logs. After clicking reports, show charts and tables with sample data.

Success Stories: Clicking a story opens detailed case study pages.

Interactive elements:

Filters (location, experience, salary, role type) update results instantly.

Cards clickable → open details.

Dashboards show real candidate/employer flows with sample content.

Forms have inline validation, OTP verification, and file upload.

Design style: Modern, clean, warm. Use muted professional blues, slates, neutrals, and teal accents (avoid red and very bright colors). Support both dark and light themes.

Accessibility: Keyboard navigation, ARIA labels, high contrast, alt text, transcripts for videos.

Performance: Fast loading, lazy image loading, responsive layouts for mobile/desktop/tablet.

Deliverables: Fully interactive clickable prototype in Lovable with all 50+ pages, every action showing real content.

Sitemap (50+ pages):
Home, About, Contact, Careers, Blog, Success Stories, FAQ, Privacy, Terms, Sitemap, Candidate Home, Browse Jobs (Software), Browse Jobs (Education), Job Detail (Software), Job Detail (Education), Apply, Candidate Signup/Login, Candidate Dashboard, My Applications, Interview Prep, Resume Builder, Skill Assessments, Mock Interviews, Salary Insights, Career Coaching, Employer Home, Post Job, Employer Signup/Login, Employer Dashboard, Shortlist, Campus Hiring, Partnerships, Pricing, Case Studies, Request Demo, Placement Services, Fast-Track, Volume Hiring, Staffing, Training, Assessments, Background Checks, Events, Workshops, Library, Downloads, Newsletter, Video Testimonials, Admin Dashboard, CRM Integrations, Reports, Audit Logs, Support, Ambassador Program, Referral Program, Partner Portal, API, Accessibility, Press, Community Guidelines.

Final Output: A 50+ page professional consultancy website where every page and option is clickable, each click leads to complete content, and the experience feels smooth, lovable, and production-ready.
